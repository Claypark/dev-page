Ounwan

One card a day, read from your Saju

Each day a card waits for you, chosen by the energy of the day. Flip it to open a reading of your day from your Saju (Korean four pillars), then swipe through cards for love, money, energy and work. Compatibility in two cards and shareable links, too.
