Cannon Holdout

One scrap cannon against the swarm

A survivors-style game where a single cannon holds off bug swarms closing in from every side. Pick from a wide range of parts and match their elements to build synergies. Spend your rewards on cannon upgrades in the hangar and push through stage after stage, region by region.

Features

  • Drag to aim your main cannon while attached parts fire on their own
  • Pick one of three upgrades every level — every run is a different build
  • 30+ parts, 15+ element synergies and 39+ bosses
  • Plays fully offline
