Sogak

Fuel today with the time you have left

A motivational countdown app that visualizes the weekends and hours you have left. Check in morning and night to mark the day, and see the time you still have with the people who matter. All your data stays on your device.

Features

  • Shows your remaining weekends, hours and days as a single number
  • 80+ life presets like 'How many more cherry blossom seasons?'
  • A life-in-weeks grid filled in from the day you were born
  • Morning and evening check-ins to record each day
