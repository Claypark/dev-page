Infinite Palace

A belt-scrolling brawler to finally move into your penthouse

In 2016, Mom spent every penny on the penthouse of 'Infinite Palace.' That winter construction stopped and the developer vanished with the money. Ten years later, the day an eviction notice hits our basement door, a contract turns up at the bottom of a box: Building 101, PH01. They say the abandoned site grows a floor every night, and the developer is hiding at the top. A pixel belt-scrolling brawler with old arcade spirit. Move left, right and up and down the street as enemies swarm in, launch them with plank combos and knock them down. Every floor brings new enemies and a boss with a story of their own, and the truth comes out piece by piece as you climb. Build your own fighting style with special gear effects, charms that work from your bag and skills learned at the gym, and fight your way up to your home at the top.

Features

  • Arcade-style belt-scroll action: move in every direction, launch enemies and juggle them in the air
  • Learn moves at the gym and slot them into buttons to build your own combos
  • Every floor brings new scenery, new traps and a new boss
  • Fight your way to the penthouse of an apartment tower left half-built for ten years
