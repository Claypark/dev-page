DoughLab

Turn dough into numbers, recipes into experiments

A baking recipe app that treats every ingredient as a percentage of the flour weight (baker's percentages). Calculate hydration and ratios at a glance, log the conditions and result of each bake, and turn trial and error into data as you perfect your own recipes. Everything stays on your device.
