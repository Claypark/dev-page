Dandori

Keep the day tight

Opening and closing checklists, shift schedules, clock-in/out and inventory — the whole day of a small store in one app. Owners see how the store is running at a glance; staff know exactly what to do today. Built for cafes, restaurants and bakeries where people work in shifts.

Features

  • Assigns each shift's tasks to whoever works today, with photo proof and manager double-checks
  • Build a schedule once and it repeats weekly; shift swaps and time off go through requests and approvals
  • Verifies clock-ins by store Wi-Fi or location and calculates pay, overtime included
  • Stock counts and orders in a simple dated table
